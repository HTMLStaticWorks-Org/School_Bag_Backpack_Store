Authenticity & Bag Care Guide

5 QUALITY PROMISES BEHIND EVERY BRAND WE STOCK.

Every partner brand in our store meets strict durability benchmarks—and following simple care habits keeps your backpack looking vibrant year after year.

1

Official 12 to 24-Month Brand Warranty

All school bags and college backpacks include manufacturer warranty coverage against seam, buckle and zipper manufacturing defects.

2

Child-Safe, Lead-Free & Odor-Free Fabrics

Preschool and primary bags use certified non-toxic dyes, food-safe PEVA lunch linings and skin-friendly air-mesh padding.

3

Proper Strap Adjustment for Posture

Adjust both shoulder straps evenly so the bottom of the backpack rests at the waistline—never hanging low below the hips.

4

Gentle Spot-Clean & Shade Drying

Wipe outer fabrics with mild soap and a damp cloth, and air-dry in the shade to preserve water-repellent PU coatings and bright prints.

5

Smart Weight Packing Order

Place heavier textbooks and laptops in the compartment closest to the spine, and lighter stationery or snack boxes in the front pockets.
