IN-STORE EXPERIENCE & STORE INFO

Our 3,500 sq. ft. colorful multi-brand showroom is organized by student age group and carry type so parents and students can test strap comfort, compare liter capacities and check laptop fit in person.

Zone 01

Kids & Ortho Fitting Bar

Complimentary posture and shoulder-strap fitting for Preschool (Ages 3–7) and Primary/Middle School (Ages 8–13) orthopedic bookbags.

Zone 02

Campus Laptop & Trolley Bay

Test 15.6" and 16" laptop sleeves, anti-theft commuter packs and 6-wheel stair-climbing trolley backpacks on our sample stair ramp.

Zone 03

Warranty & Zipper Care Desk

On-the-spot slider, buckle and strap-webbing assistance plus authorized warranty registration for all six partner backpack brands.

Zone 04

School & Bulk Order Lounge

Dedicated sample room for school principals, PTA committees and event organizers looking for custom-logo backpacks in bulk.
